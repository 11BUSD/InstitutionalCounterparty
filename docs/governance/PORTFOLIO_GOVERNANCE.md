# Portfolio Governance

Venture stages: F0 hypothesis -> F1 prototype -> F2 design partner -> F3 paying pilot -> F4 repeatable revenue -> F5 institutional scale -> F6 independent financing -> F7 growth company. Mature verticals may spin out with their own management, contracts, cap table, financing and vertical IP. The Foundry may retain transparent minority economics when warranted. Do not automatically take a fixed equity percentage from every venture.
