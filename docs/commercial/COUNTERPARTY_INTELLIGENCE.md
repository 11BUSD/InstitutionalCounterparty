# Counterparty Intelligence

Private research may cover banks, insurers, P&I, family offices, PE/VC/private credit, administrators/custodians, auction houses/private-sales networks, real-estate institutions, commodity counterparties and advisers. Historic Sotheby's, Christie's, Bonhams and Phillips data is distribution/workflow research, not product truth. Never invent wealth, AUM, authority or relationships; distinguish investor, buyer, user, sponsor, introducer and blocker; verify current roles before outreach; store private relationship intelligence outside public source.
